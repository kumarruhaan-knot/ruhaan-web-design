07FAQ

Questions owners ask first.

I already have Instagram and a Google listing. Do I need a website?

Social pages and Google listings help people discover you. A website brings your services, prices and past work together in one place. A website gives you one page you own that answers what customers ask before they contact you: what you do, your work, your prices, and how to reach you.

How much does a website cost?

A template website is S$400 for design and build. A template with bigger changes is S$500 to S$600, and a fully custom website starts from S$750. Every package includes two rounds of changes. Optional launch and setup help is S$50 to S$100. Domain, hosting and email provider charges are separate, with renewals confirmed before work starts. The price is agreed in writing before work starts.

Should I pick a template or a custom website?

Most small businesses only need a template: it is designed around what customers in your industry look for, and it is the fastest and cheapest option. Choose custom if your business does not fit any template, or you want a look nobody else has.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The website and the domain are registered to your business. You are never locked in.

What happens if something needs fixing after launch?

Message me on WhatsApp. Anything that is not working as agreed is fixed at no charge. Small updates to text, photos and hours can be arranged at any time.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work or premises, and a 15-minute conversation about your business. Help with writing the content is included.

Do you build online shops or booking systems?

The focus is on websites that present your business and bring in enquiries. If you already use a booking or payment service, your website can link to it.
